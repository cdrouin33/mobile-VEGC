Play More. Pay Less. Golf Anytime

Welcome to Vegreville Kinsmen Golf Course designed for enjoyable, fast, and affordable golf. Located on the North East Side of Vegreville, just off of the Yellowhead Highway less than an hour from the city. Our 9-hole Par 36 Layout plays up to 3370 yards and offers a fun challenge for experienced golfers while remaining welcoming for beginners and casual players. Large greens, challenging fairways, and water hazards that come into play 5 of 9 holes.

Course Layout & Scorecard

Quick reference images for the full course layout and scorecard.

Hole 1

Par: 4   •   Yardage: 375 (Red) / 395 (White) / 411 (Blue)

  • Tip: A long straight drive leaves you short of green side creek with a mid to short iron into this large green
  • Hazards: Water to the right and 100 yds short of the green
  • Green note: Large mounds protect this green

Hole 2

Par: 4   •   Yardage: 399 (Red) / 437 (White) / 476 (Blue)

  • Tip: This long par 4 requires a big drive and approach to get there in 2.
  • Hazards: Bunker short right of green and water on right side and behind
  • Green note: Protected by trees left and water behind

Hole 3

Par: 4   •   Yardage: 294 (Red) / 344 (White) / 367 (Blue)

  • Tip: Short drive or fairway wood puts you short of creek and great position for a short approach
  • Hazards: Creek protects the green from long drives
  • Green note: Large green can lead to 3 putts if not careful

Hole 4

Par: 4   •   Yardage: 292 (Red) / 321 (White) / 347 (Blue)

  • Tip: This fun hole lets you be aggresive off the tee, long drivers be cautious of creek protecting this green
  • Hazards: Creek protecting the green and water on the right can lead to trouble
  • Green note: Very protectd with wtaer in front and on the right

Hole 5

Par: 5   •   Yardage: 477 (Red) / 502 (White) / 531 (Blue)

  • Tip: This long par 5 will take everything you have off the tee for a chnace to get ther in 2
  • Hazards: Although it has a wide fairway, trees to left and right can cause trouble
  • Green note: Protected by trees and small bunker to the left

Hole 6

Par: 3   •   Yardage: 125 (Red) / 144 (White) / 175 (Blue)

  • Tip: This is the longer Par 3 on the course with a large green and mounding around
  • Hazards: Trees to the right and short left can be troublesome
  • Green note: Very well protected by mounds if you miss right or long

Hole 7

Par: 4   •   Yardage: 387 (Red) / 388 (White) / 391 (Blue)

  • Tip: This sharp dogleg left requires a well positioned tee shot
  • Hazards: Trees left in landing area if drive is short, trees right if long of the tee
  • Green note: Large greenside bail out to the right be be careful for taller rough grass there

Hole 8

Par: 5   •   Yardage: 429 (Red) / 503 (White) / 522 (Blue)

  • Tip: Long and staright and you got a good shot a birdie
  • Hazards: Mounds to the right and trees left off the tee, small greenside bunker protects left side
  • Green note: This green is very well protectd by mounding all around which makes any chip shots a challenge

Hole 9

Par: 3   •   Yardage: 103 (Red) / 141 (White) / 151 (Blue)

  • Tip: This short par 3 has water trouble all around
  • Hazards: Water protects short shots and fades to the right
  • Green note: Large bail out area left but green slopes to the water on the right
